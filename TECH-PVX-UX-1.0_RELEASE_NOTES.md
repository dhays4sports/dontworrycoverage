# TECH-PVX-UX-1.0

## Consumer experience

- Fresh technology handoffs now receive a concise first look showing the reported technology role, homeowner/renter context, and professional discount status as **Worth verifying**.
- The first-look action continues to the bounded CoverageFit discovery flow; **Ask Dylan instead** remains available as the secondary action.
- Completed Snapshots now lead with up to three evidence-backed items and plain-language reasons.
- The early readiness prompt, seven-way action grid, optional change-scope prompt, immediate property enrichment, and visible contact-plan form were removed from the initial result.
- The completed Snapshot now presents **Continue toward a quote** and **Review this with Dylan** as the two primary actions, with **Save for later** and **Keep exploring** as secondary actions.
- Contact details and exact callback scheduling remain available only after the visitor explicitly chooses Dylan’s help.

## Continuity and safety

- A fresh bootstrap is explicitly separated from an idempotent resume before the secure browser bridge opens.
- Technology professional context is carried into the completed Snapshot without determining eligibility or a discount.
- Snapshot v2 is accepted by the existing checkpoint pipeline so save, continuation, contact, and callback functions remain compatible.
- No D1 schema, RingCentral, maintenance Worker, lead-sync, consent-evidence, or secure handoff contract was removed.
